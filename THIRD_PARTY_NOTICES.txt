src-tauri/resources/provider_catalog.json, provider_status.json, oauth_providers.json, media_providers.json
  Provider names, categories, display colors, public website hosts and service
  kinds were extracted from the provider registry of 9router
  (https://github.com/decolua/9router, v0.5.99). No credentials, client
  secrets are included. provider_status.json, oauth_providers.json
  and media_providers.json additionally draw endpoint base URLs, OAuth endpoint
  URLs, public client ids of PKCE/device flows and model names from the same
  registry. No client secrets were copied.

src-tauri/src/upstream/github_compat.rs
  GitHub Copilot IDE header values follow 9Router's
  open-sse/providers/registry/github.js and open-sse/executors/github.js.
  Source: https://github.com/decolua/9router
